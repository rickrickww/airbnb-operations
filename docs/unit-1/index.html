Arrival

Getting here & getting in

Getting here

About a 6-minute drive from Vancouver International Airport (YVR). An Uber or Lyft costs around $20–30 CAD. Gilmore Crescent is off No. 4 Road.

Open in Maps

Parking

There is one shared parking spot on the property, outlined in the photo below.

If it's already taken, please park on the road. There is free street parking on Gilmore Crescent, Finlayson Drive, and No. 4 Road.

Please don't block the driveway or a neighbour's access.

Getting in

  1. Unit 1 is on the right side of the house. Go through the gate on the right and follow the path along the side. There are stairs up to the suite.
  2. On the keypad, enter your passcode: the last 4 digits of the phone number on your booking.
  3. Press the ✔️ button to unlock.
  4. To lock the door, hold the ✔️ button for 3 seconds.

Self check-in is available from 4:00 PM until 2:00 AM. Can't find the entrance? Message us in the Airbnb app and we'll guide you in.

Arriving early or leaving late?

Luggage drop-off before check-in or after check-out is available on request, and late check-out may be possible. Please message us in advance.

Before you go

By 10:00 AM, please

  • Put used cups and dishes in the dishwasher. No need to run it.
  • Leave used towels and linens in the bathroom basket.
  • Sort your rubbish into garbage, recycling, and compost.
  • Turn off all lights, the thermostat, and the air conditioner.
  • Check for your belongings.
  • Lock the door: hold the ✔️ button for 3 seconds.

House rules

Keeping everyone comfortable

  • Quiet hours are 11:00 PM to 7:00 AM, outdoors too.
  • No smoking or vaping indoors or near doors and windows. Signs of smoking lead to a deep-cleaning fee.
  • No pets, parties, events, or drug use.
  • Please take off your shoes at the door. Clean slippers are provided and disinfected between guests.
  • Flush toilet paper only. No wipes, paper towels, or feminine products.
  • Please don't move or rearrange the furniture.
  • Never leave cooking unattended, and take care with candles or any open flame.
  • Please tell us about any damage or maintenance issue right away so we can fix it.
  • No commercial photography or filming without written permission.

The suite

How everything works

Where you'll sleep

  • Bedroom 1: one queen bed
  • Bedroom 2: one queen bed
  • Living room: a sofa bed

Both bedrooms have room-darkening shades, a closet, and hangers. Extra pillows and blankets are provided.

Wi-Fi

The network name and password are in the Airbnb app, in your reservation details.

The connection is fast, around 400 Mbps, so video calls and 4K streaming are no problem. If the internet drops, message us and we'll sort it out.

Heating

The suite has radiant heating, controlled by the Google Nest thermostat on the wall. Turn the ring to set the temperature you'd like.

A portable heater is also provided if you'd like extra warmth.

Cooling

For the air conditioning, use the remote on the TV counter. That remote controls the air conditioner only, not the heating.

A ceiling fan and portable fans are also available.

The windows are casement style. Please close and lock them when you go out.

Bathroom

One towel per guest, toilet paper, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, and a hair dryer. Need more towels? Just ask.

Using the sofa bed

The living room sofa converts to a bed. Pull the armrests up and slide them out to remove them. Extra pillows and blankets are provided.

Laundry

A washer and dryer are available in a shared space for $15 per use. Please message us in advance to arrange access. A drying rack and an iron are in the suite.

TV

The 58" smart TV has Netflix, Disney+, and YouTube. Sign in with your own accounts, and please sign out before you leave.

Security cameras

For everyone's safety, cameras record outside the property 24/7. There are no cameras inside the suite.

Getting around

  • Airport (YVR): about 6 minutes by car, around $20–30 CAD by Uber or Lyft.
  • SkyTrain: Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line is about a 16-minute walk, with trains to the airport, Richmond Centre, and downtown Vancouver.

Things to do

  • Tait Waterfront Park: a 5-minute walk away, with paths along the water. Lime e-scooters and e-bikes can be rented here.
  • River Road Dyke Trail: a scenic walk along the river, good for a morning stroll.
  • Richmond Night Market: a seasonal outdoor market with food stalls and entertainment, near Bridgeport Station.
  • Richmond Centre: the main shopping mall, easy to reach by SkyTrain.

Before you review

A small favour

★★★★★

We're a small, family-run place, and your review genuinely matters to us.

On Airbnb, a 5-star rating simply means the stay was as described. It isn't reserved for something extraordinary. Ratings below that are treated as a problem with the listing, so even 4 stars sets us back quite a bit.

So if anything at all falls short — a missing towel, something not working, a question about the suite — please message us during your stay rather than after. We'd much rather fix it while you're still here.

Thank you for helping us keep this a welcoming place for the next guest.
